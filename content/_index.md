[Tonny Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T._A._Springer), whose work shaped modern algebra and representation theory, would have celebrated his 100th birthday on February 13, 2026.
In honor of his work,
we organize a 1-day workshop
dedicated to his legacy
on modern mathematics.

The workshop will take place on November 6, 2026.

There is no registration.
